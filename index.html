02 · SUBMIT + STATUS

File the link, watch the credit

After posting, hand the link back to your agent. Your agent files it on-chain through the inbox contract. The scanner runs in rounds: it opens every filed link, checks the words, the account age and the posting time, blocks double claims, then credits wallets in one batch. Credits land in the claim contract, ready for your agent to redeem.
